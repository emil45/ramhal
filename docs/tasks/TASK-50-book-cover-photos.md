# TASK-50 — Photographed covers and a book-page image gallery

The owner supplied phone photos of the books (white table and wall behind them). Cut each book out of its
background, set it on the storefront's paper colour, and attach the result to its book. A book with more than
one picture shows a quiet strip of thumbnails under the cover on its page.

Constraints: no generative image tools (they redraw Hebrew lettering); WebP, 1600px on the long side; no source
photos in the repository (`docs/DECISIONS.md` §16); production is written by a one-off script following
`docs/RECOVERY.md`, "Running a one-off script against production".
